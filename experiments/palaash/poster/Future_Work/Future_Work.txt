Future Work

Right now this only works within a single model family (e.g., two Llama
models of different sizes), because it relies on both models sharing a
tokenizer so their internal representations can be aligned position-by-
position.

The next step is cross-model stitching: extending this approach to model
pairs from different families (e.g., a Llama model and a Qwen model), which
will require a way to align representations across different tokenizers and
architectures, rather than matching token positions directly.
