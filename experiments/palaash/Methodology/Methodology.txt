Methodology
(diagram: how_the_stitch_works.png)

We build a hybrid model. The large model's early layers read the prompt; a
small trained adapter translates its internal representation into the small
model's representation space; the small model's later layers take over from
there and produce the final answer. The large model's early layers are never
fine-tuned -- only the adapter and the small model's later layers are
trained.

Training uses knowledge distillation. For each training prompt, the large
model's full forward pass (run once, offline) produces its own answer
distribution. The adapter and the small model's later layers are then trained
so that the hybrid model's answer distribution matches it as closely as
possible.

At answer time, only the large model's early layers and the small model's
later layers actually run -- the large model's full forward pass is only
ever needed once, during training, to build the target the adapter learns to
match.
